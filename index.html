ICME workshop information

The workshop

One model.
Shared progress.

Unified multimodal models both understand and generate text, images, video and audio. Reinforcement learning can improve these abilities together, but only if the reward deserves to be trusted.

UniRL brings together researchers building RL-tuned generative and unified models with the multimedia community studying perceptual quality, audio, speech and evaluation. We ask how to turn a model’s understanding into a useful signal for its generation, and its generation into a learning signal for understanding, without teaching the model to fool its own judge.

How should we design RL so that rewards are trustworthy and gains transfer between understanding and generation?

What is the policy?

Connect discrete text plans with continuous visual and audio trajectories.

Who gives the reward?

Align learned and verifiable rewards with human judgments of media quality.

Does RL create synergy?

Measure shared improvement beyond a model agreeing with itself.

Research scope

Three connected tracks

From policy design to reward design to evidence of real progress.

Algorithms

RL for unified & generative models

Learning across architectures, modalities and generation steps.

  • GRPO, PPO, DPO and their variants
  • Autoregressive, diffusion, flow, masked and hybrid models
  • Exploration and credit assignment
  • Joint understanding and generation
  • Efficient online RL for image, video and audio

Rewards

Rewards & verifiers

Feedback that reflects what people value in generated media.

  • Unified and reasoning reward models
  • Rubric- and preference-based rewards
  • Intrinsic and self-rewarding signals
  • Verifiers for counting, OCR and spatial relations
  • Perceptual quality, video, audio and speech rewards

Reliability

Reliability & evaluation

Distinguishing genuine gains from reward exploitation.

  • Reward hacking and over-optimization
  • Diversity collapse and generation artifacts
  • Held-out and human evaluation
  • Transfer between understanding and generation
  • Benchmarks, safety and provenance

Contribute to UniRL

Call for Papers

Draft call, subject to workshop acceptance and final ICME author guidelines.

Submissions not yet open

We invite research on making RL for generative and unified multimodal models effective, trustworthy and synergistic.

Contributions may address any of the three research tracks, including reasoning-driven generation and editing, interleaved outputs, multimodal reward modeling, reward robustness, evaluation and online RL efficiency.

Paper format

Regular research papers in the ICME 2027 workshop format. The page limit, author instructions and proceedings details will be announced once confirmed.

Review process

The proposed process is double-blind review on OpenReview, with at least three reviews per paper. A non-conflicted senior organizer will handle submissions involving an organizer.

Presentation

Accepted work will be presented as posters, with six papers selected for five-minute spotlights based on review scores and program balance.

Paper submission deadline

April 9, 2027

The submission portal will be announced after workshop acceptance.
